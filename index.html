What couldn’t be matched one to one

  • Check-in cards other than high confidence: the Figma switcher only links these states to the lock screen. To get the card, the script opens the lock-screen link and taps the notification. The high confidence card uses the “Check-in card” direct link.
  • Reschedule review: both show the review after tapping the first time slot, but the slots differ. Claude’s first slot is Fri, 20 Nov, 8:00. Figma’s is Wed, 18 Nov, 09:00.
  • Only in Figma Make: High confidence / no note, Ready and Failure as separate direct states, Waiting (long), lock screens for every scenario, and the follow-up screens Rescheduled, Book again picker and review, Rebooked, Remind later, Reminder confirmation, Timing submitted and Upcoming.
  • Only in Claude: #booking-long. It wasn’t in the list, but it matches Figma’s “Waiting (long)”.
